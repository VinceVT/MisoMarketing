1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Miso Marketing Group ("Miso Marketing," "we," "us," or "our") handles personal information in connection with this website (the "Site"). We are a social media marketing, photography, and videography studio based in San Jose, California.

This policy covers the Site only. If you become a client, the handling of your business information, your customers' information, and the content we produce for you is governed by the separate written services agreement we sign with you. Where that agreement and this policy conflict, the services agreement controls for client matters.

2. What we collect

Information you choose to give us

If you use the contact form, email us, or message us on social media, we receive whatever you include — typically your name, your email address, your restaurant or business name, and the content of your message. You decide what goes in that message. Please do not send us passwords, payment card numbers, government ID numbers, health information, or anyone else's personal information through the Site.

Information collected automatically

The Site is served as static files from Amazon Web Services. Like essentially every web host, AWS may record standard server-log information when a page or image is requested, including your IP address, the time of the request, the file requested, your browser's user-agent string, and the referring page. We use this only in aggregate, for security and reliability, and we do not use it to build a profile of you.

Fonts loaded from Google

The Site loads typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). To deliver a font, your browser contacts Google directly, which means Google receives your IP address and basic request information. We do not control and do not receive that data. Google's handling of it is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we do not collect

  • We do not run Google Analytics or any other analytics product on this Site.
  • We do not run advertising pixels, retargeting tags, or conversion trackers on this Site.
  • We do not set cookies.
  • We do not ask for or process payments through this Site.
  • We do not have user accounts, logins, or stored profiles.

3. How the contact form works

This is worth stating plainly, because it is unusual and it is in your favor. The "Send Message" button on our contact form does not transmit your entry to a server we control. It assembles what you typed into a pre-addressed draft and hands it to the email program on your own device. Nothing reaches us unless you then send that draft yourself. If you close the draft, we never see it.

Once you do send it, the message travels through your email provider and arrives in our business inbox, which is hosted on Google Workspace / Gmail. From that point it is an ordinary email in our inbox and is covered by the rest of this policy.

4. How we use information

We use the information you send us to:

  • respond to your inquiry and answer questions about our services;
  • prepare a proposal, quote, or scope of work;
  • schedule and carry out shoots, calls, and meetings;
  • keep business records, including invoices and correspondence;
  • comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations; and
  • protect our rights, or respond to a legal claim or lawful request.

We do not use your information to train machine-learning models, and we do not add you to a marketing list from a contact-form inquiry alone. If we ever start a newsletter, it will be opt-in and every message will carry an unsubscribe link.

5. Cookies and tracking

We do not set cookies on this Site, and we do not use local storage to track you across visits. Because we set no cookies and run no trackers, there is no cookie banner and nothing to opt out of here.

Your browser may still cache files, and the third parties described above (AWS, Google Fonts) receive request data as part of delivering the page. If we add any tracking technology in the future, we will update this section and the "Last updated" date before it goes live.

6. Do Not Track

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. There is no common industry standard for how a site should respond to one. Because this Site does not track visitors across time or across third-party websites in the first place, our behavior is the same whether or not a DNT signal is present. We also do not permit third parties to collect personally identifiable information about your online activities over time and across different websites when you visit this Site.

7. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose information only as follows:

  • Service providers. Vendors that run our infrastructure, under terms that limit them to that purpose — currently our web host (Amazon Web Services), our email and document provider (Google Workspace), and font delivery (Google Fonts). If we add a scheduling, invoicing, or e-signature tool for client work, it will be added to this list.
  • Professional advisers. Our accountant, insurer, or attorney, when needed and under a duty of confidentiality.
  • Legal requirements. If we are required to by subpoena, court order, or other valid legal process, or where we believe disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect the safety, rights, or property of any person.
  • Business transfer. If the business is sold, merged, or reorganized, business records may transfer to the successor, which would remain bound by this policy for information collected before the transfer.

8. How long we keep it

We keep inquiry emails for as long as it takes to respond and for a reasonable period afterward in case you follow up — generally no more than two years for inquiries that do not become projects. Records tied to paid work (contracts, invoices, correspondence) are kept for at least four years to meet tax, accounting, and limitations-period requirements. You can ask us to delete an inquiry sooner; see Your choices and rights.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, including multi-factor authentication on our email and cloud accounts and limiting access to the two of us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, and email in particular is not a secure channel. Please do not send us sensitive information by email.

10. Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what personal information of yours we hold, correct it, or delete it. Email misomarketinggroup@gmail.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject line and tell us what you want done.

We honor these requests as a matter of practice, whether or not a particular law requires us to. We will respond within 45 days. We may need to verify that the request really comes from you — usually by replying to the email address we already have on file. We will not discriminate against you for making a request. We may decline to delete information we are required to keep for tax, accounting, or legal-defense reasons, and we will tell you if that is the case.

11. California privacy rights

We are a small business based in California. The California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA, applies to businesses above certain revenue and data-volume thresholds, and we do not currently meet them. We are telling you this rather than implying a legal status we do not have. Regardless, the access, correction, and deletion rights described in section 10 are available to you, and the categories of personal information we collect, the purposes we collect them for, and the categories of recipients are set out in sections 2, 4, and 7 above.

Shine the Light. California Civil Code § 1798.83 lets California residents ask a business once a year for a list of the personal information it disclosed to third parties for those third parties' own direct-marketing purposes. We do not make any such disclosures. You may still send the request to the email address in section 17 and we will confirm that in writing.

Notice at collection. At the point where this Site invites you to enter your information — the contact form — we link to this policy so you can see what is collected and why before you submit anything.

12. Photography and client work

Our portfolio shows photographs and video we produced for restaurants and other food businesses. These images may include food, interiors, staff, and occasionally guests or models. We publish this work with the permission of the business that engaged us, and we obtain releases where a person is recognizably featured.

If you appear in an image on this Site, or own a property or dish shown in one, and you want it removed, email misomarketinggroup@gmail.com with enough detail to identify the image. We will review the request promptly and, where we cannot verify a release, take the image down while we look into it.

13. Children

This Site is directed to restaurant owners and operators, not to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 as defined by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Visitors outside the United States

We operate in the United States and our services are offered to businesses in the United States. If you visit this Site from outside the U.S., understand that any information you send us will be transferred to, stored in, and processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your country. By contacting us you consent to that transfer.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom and believe the GDPR applies to our handling of your information, our lawful bases would be your consent (for an inquiry you choose to send) and our legitimate interest in responding to business inquiries and keeping ordinary business records. You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing, by emailing us at the address below.

15. Third-party sites

This Site links to our profiles on Instagram and TikTok, and may link to clients' websites or ordering platforms. We do not control those services and this policy does not apply to them. Once you follow a link, you are subject to that company's own privacy policy and tracking practices, which are typically far more extensive than ours.

We may update this policy as the business or the Site changes. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes — for example, if we begin collecting information through a hosted form or add analytics — will be reflected here before the change takes effect. Your continued use of the Site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
