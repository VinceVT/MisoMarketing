Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 15, 2026

The short version

We want everyone to be able to use this site. We build toward WCAG 2.1 Level AA and we have fixed a number of specific issues, but we have not had a formal audit, so we describe ourselves as partially conformant and we list what we know is still imperfect. If anything here gets in your way, email us and we will give you the information another way — and fix the problem.

Our commitment

Miso Marketing Group is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use screen readers, magnification, speech input, keyboard-only navigation, or who are sensitive to motion. Accessibility is not a one-time project; we treat it as ongoing maintenance.

Conformance status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) define requirements for making web content more accessible. This site aims to conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

This site is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 Level AA. "Partially conformant" means that some parts of the content do not yet fully conform. We have not commissioned an independent third-party audit, and we would rather say so than claim a level of conformance we have not verified.

What we have done

Specific measures currently in place on this site:

  • A "skip to content" link so keyboard users can bypass the navigation.
  • Navigation and buttons that are reachable and operable with a keyboard alone, in a logical order.
  • A visible focus indicator on every interactive element, so you can always see where you are.
  • Text labels on every contact-form field, programmatically associated with the field, rather than placeholder text alone.
  • The mobile menu button reports its open or closed state to assistive technology.
  • Alternative text on portfolio and content images; purely decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Full support for the prefers-reduced-motion setting. If your operating system is set to reduce motion, the scrolling service marquee stops, the floating and fade-in animations are disabled, and smooth scrolling is turned off.
  • Text and interface colors reviewed against the WCAG 4.5:1 contrast minimum, with a deeper red introduced for small text and for white text on a red background.
  • Semantic HTML landmarks and a single, ordered heading structure on each page.
  • A responsive layout that works down to 320px wide and reflows at 200% browser zoom without loss of content.
  • The page language is declared, and the site works without JavaScript on these legal pages.

Known limitations

We are aware of the following, and we are working on them. If you hit something not on this list, please tell us.

  • Portfolio image descriptions. Alternative text on gallery photographs is currently short and categorical ("Food Photography," "Plating") rather than a full description of each dish or scene. We are rewriting these.
  • No independent audit. Our review has been internal — automated checks plus manual keyboard and screen-reader testing. We have not had the site tested by an external accessibility specialist or by a panel of users with disabilities.
  • Decorative animation. The site still uses motion by default for visitors who have not set a reduced-motion preference. There is no in-page control to pause the service marquee independently of that system setting.
  • Third-party content. We link to Instagram and TikTok. We do not control the accessibility of those platforms.
  • The brief loading animation. The site shows a short branded loading screen before content appears. It is disabled for reduced-motion users but may still be disorienting on a slow connection.

Getting help, and alternative access

If any part of this site is not usable for you, you do not have to work around it. Email misomarketinggroup@gmail.com with "Accessibility" in the subject line and we will give you the same information in another format — plain text, a phone call, or a conversation over email — and help you with anything the site would otherwise have handled, including requesting a quote.

You will never be asked to use an inaccessible feature to reach us, and you will not be charged anything extra for assistance.

Feedback

We welcome reports of accessibility barriers. Please tell us the page, what you were trying to do, what happened, and the browser and assistive technology you were using, if you know. Send it to misomarketinggroup@gmail.com.

We aim to acknowledge accessibility feedback within 5 business days and to describe how and when we will address it. Barriers that block someone from contacting us or understanding our services are treated as urgent.

Technical information

Accessibility of this site relies on the following technologies: HTML, CSS, SVG, and — on the main page — JavaScript. These legal pages are plain HTML and work with JavaScript disabled. The main page requires JavaScript to render.

The site is designed to work with current versions of major browsers and with common assistive technologies, including screen readers, screen magnifiers, and speech recognition software.

How we assessed this site

Miso Marketing Group assessed the accessibility of this site by self-evaluation, combining automated accessibility checks with manual testing: keyboard-only navigation of every interactive element, inspection of the heading and landmark structure, verification of form-field labeling, calculation of color-contrast ratios against the WCAG 4.5:1 and 3:1 thresholds, and testing with the reduced-motion system preference enabled.
